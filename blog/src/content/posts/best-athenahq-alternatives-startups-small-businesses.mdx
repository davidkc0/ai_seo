## When AthenaHQ may still be right

If you have a larger team, larger budget, and a more mature AI-search operation, AthenaHQ may be worth evaluating.

That is not the same as saying every team should start there.

For most startups and local businesses, the right first move is lower-friction:

1. Run the website audit.
2. Track a small set of buyer queries.
3. Identify the competitors AI already trusts.
4. Ship fixes.
5. Rerun and measure.
