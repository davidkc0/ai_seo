AI search visibility is turning into another enterprise-tax category.

Profound and AthenaHQ are building serious platforms, but most startups and small businesses do not need a huge platform just to answer:

- Does ChatGPT recommend us?
- Who shows up instead?
- Is our website clear enough to cite?
- What should we fix next?

That is why I built Illusion: AI search monitoring plus a free AI website analyzer for teams that want the practical version first.

Run the audit: https://www.illusion.ai/analyze

### Reddit / Indie Hackers

I built a free AI website analyzer because most “AI SEO” advice starts too late.

Before you pay for a platform or agency, your site needs to answer the boring questions:

- what do you do?
- who do you serve?
- where do you operate?
- why should customers trust you?
- can search engines and AI crawlers read the page?
- do you have service pages, FAQs, schema, and reviews?

Illusion checks those basics and returns a plain-English fix list. It is especially meant for startups, agencies, and small local businesses that are getting priced out of AI SEO tools.

Free audit: https://www.illusion.ai/analyze
