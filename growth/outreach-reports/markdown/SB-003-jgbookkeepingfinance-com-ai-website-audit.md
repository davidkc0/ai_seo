## Executive Summary

The site is crawlable, but the highest-leverage fixes are about clarity, trust, and structured business signals. Prioritize the high-severity items first so visitors and AI answer engines can quickly understand who the business serves and how to contact it.

## Top Findings

### 1. Make the title tag more descriptive

- Severity: medium
- Category: seo
- Evidence: Current title: "Hostinger Horizons"
- Fix: Expand the title with the main service and location or target customer.
- Expected impact: Improves search snippets and gives AI systems a clearer label for the business.
- Effort: low

### 2. Add a meta description

- Severity: medium
- Category: seo
- Evidence: The homepage has no meta description.
- Fix: Add a one-sentence description of who you help, what you do, and how to contact you.
- Expected impact: Improves click appeal in search and gives AI summarizers a clean business description.
- Effort: low

### 3. Add one plain-language H1

- Severity: high
- Category: content
- Evidence: The homepage does not have a visible H1 heading.
- Fix: Use one headline that says exactly what the business is and who it serves.
- Expected impact: Makes the page easier for visitors and answer engines to classify.
- Effort: low

### 4. Add a strong contact call to action

- Severity: high
- Category: user_experience
- Evidence: The crawler did not find obvious phrases like book, call, schedule, contact, or get a quote.
- Fix: Place a visible CTA near the top of the homepage and repeat it on service pages.
- Expected impact: More visitors will know the next step, and AI summaries can mention how to engage the business.
- Effort: low

### 5. Show contact information in crawlable text

- Severity: high
- Category: local_seo
- Evidence: No email address or US-style phone number was found in the crawled text.
- Fix: Add phone/email details in the header, footer, or contact section as real text, not only inside an image.
- Expected impact: Builds trust for local customers and gives search engines verifiable business signals.
- Effort: low

### 6. Add LocalBusiness or ProfessionalService schema

- Severity: high
- Category: structured_data
- Evidence: Detected schema types: none
- Fix: Add JSON-LD with business name, URL, phone, address/service area, opening hours, and sameAs profiles.
- Expected impact: Structured data helps Google and AI systems confidently identify the business entity.
- Effort: medium

### 7. Add local service-area signals

- Severity: medium
- Category: local_seo
- Evidence: The crawled pages did not show strong location or service-area language.
- Fix: Mention the city, region, or service area in headings, footer copy, and service descriptions.
- Expected impact: Local customers and local-intent searches need geographic confidence.
- Effort: low

### 8. Add reviews or testimonials

- Severity: medium
- Category: user_experience
- Evidence: No review/testimonial language was detected.
- Fix: Add 2-4 real customer testimonials, review snippets, or links to Google Business reviews.
- Expected impact: Trust proof matters for humans and gives AI systems third-party-style evidence to summarize.
- Effort: low

### 9. Add a short FAQ section

- Severity: medium
- Category: ai_search
- Evidence: No FAQ-style content was detected.
- Fix: Add answers to common buyer questions: pricing, turnaround time, who you serve, and when to contact you.
- Expected impact: Question-answer content maps directly to how people ask ChatGPT, Claude, and Google AI Overviews.
- Effort: low
