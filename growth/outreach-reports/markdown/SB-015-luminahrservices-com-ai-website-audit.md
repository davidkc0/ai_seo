## Executive Summary

The site is crawlable, but the highest-leverage fixes are about clarity, trust, and structured business signals. Prioritize the high-severity items first so visitors and AI answer engines can quickly understand who the business serves and how to contact it.

## Top Findings

### 1. Show contact information in crawlable text

- Severity: high
- Category: local_seo
- Evidence: No email address or US-style phone number was found in the crawled text.
- Fix: Add phone/email details in the header, footer, or contact section as real text, not only inside an image.
- Expected impact: Builds trust for local customers and gives search engines verifiable business signals.
- Effort: low

### 2. Add LocalBusiness or ProfessionalService schema

- Severity: high
- Category: structured_data
- Evidence: Detected schema types: BreadcrumbList, EntryPoint, ListItem, PropertyValueSpecification, ReadAction, SearchAction, WebPage, WebSite
- Fix: Add JSON-LD with business name, URL, phone, address/service area, opening hours, and sameAs profiles.
- Expected impact: Structured data helps Google and AI systems confidently identify the business entity.
- Effort: medium

### 3. Add a short FAQ section

- Severity: medium
- Category: ai_search
- Evidence: No FAQ-style content was detected.
- Fix: Add answers to common buyer questions: pricing, turnaround time, who you serve, and when to contact you.
- Expected impact: Question-answer content maps directly to how people ask ChatGPT, Claude, and Google AI Overviews.
- Effort: low
