## Executive Summary

The site has a solid foundation for customers and AI search. The next gains come from adding more specific service, location, FAQ, and trust proof signals.

## Top Findings

### 1. Add local service-area signals

- Severity: medium
- Category: local_seo
- Evidence: The crawled pages did not show strong location or service-area language.
- Fix: Mention the city, region, or service area in headings, footer copy, and service descriptions.
- Expected impact: Local customers and local-intent searches need geographic confidence.
- Effort: low

### 2. Add reviews or testimonials

- Severity: medium
- Category: user_experience
- Evidence: No review/testimonial language was detected.
- Fix: Add 2-4 real customer testimonials, review snippets, or links to Google Business reviews.
- Expected impact: Trust proof matters for humans and gives AI systems third-party-style evidence to summarize.
- Effort: low

### 3. Improve image alt text coverage

- Severity: low
- Category: seo
- Evidence: 12/20 crawled images had alt text.
- Fix: Add descriptive alt text to important images, especially logos, team photos, service graphics, and trust badges.
- Expected impact: Improves accessibility and gives crawlers more context.
- Effort: low

To show up in those answers, your website needs to make your business easy to understand: clear services, location or audience signals, trust proof, structured content, and obvious next steps.

Illusion helps small businesses answer a simple question: when potential customers ask Google or AI tools for recommendations, does your business show up - or do your competitors? The first step is making sure your website clearly explains what you do, who you help, why someone should trust you, and how to contact you.
