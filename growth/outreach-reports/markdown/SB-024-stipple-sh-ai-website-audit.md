## Executive Summary

The site is crawlable, but the highest-leverage fixes are about clarity, trust, and structured business signals. Prioritize the high-severity items first so visitors and AI answer engines can quickly understand who the business serves and how to contact it.

## Top Findings

### 1. Add LocalBusiness or ProfessionalService schema

- Severity: high
- Category: structured_data
- Evidence: Detected schema types: none
- Fix: Add JSON-LD with business name, URL, phone, address/service area, opening hours, and sameAs profiles.
- Expected impact: Structured data helps Google and AI systems confidently identify the business entity.
- Effort: medium

### 2. Create dedicated service pages

- Severity: medium
- Category: content
- Evidence: The crawl did not find obvious service pages.
- Fix: Create one page per main service with who it is for, what is included, pricing cues, FAQs, and a CTA.
- Expected impact: Specific service pages give AI systems quotable evidence and give customers a clearer path.
- Effort: medium

### 3. Add local service-area signals

- Severity: medium
- Category: local_seo
- Evidence: The crawled pages did not show strong location or service-area language.
- Fix: Mention the city, region, or service area in headings, footer copy, and service descriptions.
- Expected impact: Local customers and local-intent searches need geographic confidence.
- Effort: low

### 4. Publish or expose a sitemap

- Severity: low
- Category: technical
- Evidence: No sitemap URLs were found from /sitemap.xml or robots.txt.
- Fix: Generate a sitemap.xml and reference it from robots.txt.
- Expected impact: Helps search engines and AI retrieval systems discover important pages.
- Effort: low
